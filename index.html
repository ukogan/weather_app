Understanding Solfege Systems

Fixed Do

Each syllable corresponds to a specific pitch, regardless of key:

  • Do = C (always)
  • Re = D (always)
  • Mi = E (always)
  • Fa = F (always)
  • Sol = G (always)
  • La = A (always)
  • Ti = B (always)

Common in France, Italy, Spain, and Latin America

Movable Do

Do is always the tonic (1st note) of the current key:

  • Do = 1st scale degree (tonic)
  • Re = 2nd scale degree
  • Mi = 3rd scale degree
  • Fa = 4th scale degree
  • Sol = 5th scale degree
  • La = 6th scale degree
  • Ti = 7th scale degree

Common in USA, UK, Germany, and for ear training
